Part A: Grammar Application (文法應用)

請根據第一條條件句 (First Conditional) 與不確定語氣助動詞的語法規則，選出最正確的答案。

1. If the weather is warm tomorrow, the snow ________ very fast. Certainty
正確答案：B。If引導的從屬子句使用現在簡單式(is)，主要子句表達預期發生的結果，應使用「will + 原形動詞」。
2. Many sea animals ________ their homes if humans continue to pollute the oceans. Certainty
正確答案：C。If引導的條件子句在後面(if humans continue...)，主要子句放在前面，需使用「will + 原形動詞」表示確定會發生的結果。
3. If you do not recycle that plastic bottle, it ________ in the environment for hundreds of years. Certainty
正確答案：C。條件子句使用現在簡單式的否定型(do not recycle)，主要子句用will搭配原形動詞表示必然發生的後果。
4. Scientists are not 100% sure, but global warming ________ cause more storms in the future. Possibility
正確答案：C。題目提及「not 100% sure」(非百分之百確定)，表示一種未知的可能性，故應選代表可能性的助動詞 might。
5. Choose the sentence with the CORRECT punctuation (標點符號).
正確答案：B。當 If 子句放在句首時，與主要子句之間必須加上逗號(,)隔開；若主要子句在前，則不需加逗號。
6. If everyone ________ public transportation, the air will become much cleaner. Certainty
正確答案：A。If引導的條件子句中要使用現在簡單式代替未來式。主詞 everyone 視為單數，因此動詞 use 要加 s 變成 uses。
7. If we do not protect the forests, wild animals ________ have enough food to eat. Certainty
正確答案：B。主要子句表否定且具確定性，使用 won't (will not) 搭配原形動詞 have。
8. Look at the cloudy sky! If it rains, the outdoor Earth Day party ________ be canceled, but they haven't decided yet. Possibility
正確答案：B。後半句提到「they haven't decided yet」(他們還沒決定)，表示這件事是不確定的，所以選表示可能性的助動詞 may。
9. If the sea level ________ too much, coastal cities will face severe floods. Certainty
正確答案：B。If 子句內使用現在簡單式。主詞 the sea level（海平面）是不可數名詞，視為單數，動詞用 rises。
10. If we plant more trees, they ________ carbon dioxide from the air. Certainty
正確答案：C。主要子句說明種樹之後會帶來的必然結果，應使用第一條件句的標準結構「will + 原形動詞」。
11. There is a small chance, but we ________ save the endangered birds if we act immediately. Possibility
正確答案：B。關鍵詞「There is a small chance」（有一絲機會），代表這並非百分之百保證，而是一種弱可能性，應選 could。
12. If people ________ less meat, it will help reduce green house gases. Certainty
正確答案：A。If引導的條件子句主詞為複數名詞 people，因此動詞使用現在簡單式的原形動詞 eat。
13. Choose the sentence that shows the result is NOT 100% certain. (選出結果「非百分之百確定」的句子)
正確答案：B。助動詞 may 代表「可能（不確定）」，而 A、C、D 選項皆表達極高的肯定或必然規則。
14. If the factory ________ dumping dirty water into the river, the fish will recover. Certainty
正確答案：C。主詞 the factory 是單數名詞，所以在 If 條件子句的現在簡單式中，動詞須為單數型態 stops。
15. If we don't start cleaning the beach, the trash ________ be washed into the ocean during the next storm, but we don't know the exact path of the wind. Possibility
正確答案：B。因為有「we don't know the exact path...」（我們不知道精確的路徑），這代表未知的可能性，所以最適合使用 might。
16. The Arctic animals might lose their habitats if the world ________ warmer. Possibility
正確答：B。雖然主要子句用了 might 表可能性，但 if 引導的條件子句規則不變，依然使用現在簡單式。主詞 the world 為單數，故選 gets。
17. Identify the grammatically CORRECT sentence below.
正確答案：B。If 子句不可使用未來式 will，應使用現在簡單式 turn off；主要子句用 will save。且句首有 If，中間有逗號。
18. If a student ________ paper bags instead of plastic ones, they help the trees. Certainty
正確答案：A。主詞 a student 為單數名詞，If 條件子句內使用現在簡單式單數動詞 uses。
19. If you leave the tap running while brushing your teeth, you ________ tons of fresh water. Certainty
正確答案：B。開水龍頭刷牙必然會造成浪費，這是客觀預測的結果，因此主要子句用「will + 原形動詞」。
20. Coral reefs ________ die if the seawater temperature continues to go up, but scientists are hoping some corals can adapt. Possibility
正確答案：A。後半句說科學家希望珊瑚能適應，表示死去的結果並非绝对，而是一種可能發生的威脅，因此選 might。

Part B: Reading Comprehension 1 (閱讀理解一)

The Green Campus Project

Greenwood Elementary School is starting a new project next Monday. The students want to make their school clean and green. If they collect 500 kilograms of paper and plastic bottles in one month, the principal will throw a big "Zero-Waste Pizza Party" for the whole school.

The science teacher, Mr. Green, explains that if students recycle more, less garbage will go into the local landfill. He tells the class, "If we work together, we will definitely reach our goal. However, if we do not sort our trash correctly, the recycling factory might not accept our boxes. We must be very careful."

Many students are excited. Lily, a grade 5 student, says, "If my friends and I bring old newspapers from home, we will reach the goal faster. If we succeed, our campus will become a model for other schools in the city!"
21. According to the story, what will happen if the students successfully collect 500 kilograms of recyclable materials? Inference
正確答案：C。根據文章第一段「...the principal will throw a big 'Zero-Waste Pizza Party' for the whole school.」可推得此結果。
22. What is a possible negative outcome if students are careless when sorting the trash? Inference
正確答案：B。文章第二段提到：「...if we do not sort our trash correctly, the recycling factory might not accept our boxes.」由此可知。
23. Based on Mr. Green's words, what is a CERTAIN result of recycling more? Inference
正確答案：A。文中第二段指出「if students recycle more, less garbage will go into the local landfill.」使用will表示確定的結果。
24. What will happen if Lily and her friends bring newspapers from home? Inference
正確答案：C。第三段Lily提到「If my friends and I bring old newspapers from home, we will reach the goal faster.」。
25. Which statement can be inferred (推論) from the reading passage? Inference
正確答案：C。整篇文章利用多個條件句（if...）說明計畫成敗完全取決於學生的行動（是否分類正確、是否帶報紙、是否合作）。

Part C: Reading Comprehension 2 (閱讀理解二)

The Uncertain Future of Sea Turtles

Sea turtles are beautiful marine animals, but they face many dangers today. Because of global warming, ocean levels are rising, and sandy beaches are disappearing. Sea turtles lay their eggs on these beaches. If their nesting beaches disappear, sea turtles might not find a safe place to lay eggs, which could lead to a drop in their population.

Another big threat is plastic pollution. Turtles often mistake plastic bags for jellyfish, which is their favorite food. If a turtle eats plastic, it may become very sick or even die.

Marine scientists say that if we reduce plastic waste on land, we will protect these ancient creatures. However, if governments do not create stronger laws to stop ocean pollution soon, some species of sea turtles could disappear completely within fifty years. Their survival is in our hands.
26. Why are disappearing beaches a threat to sea turtles according to the text? Inference
正確答案：B。第一段指出「If their nesting beaches disappear, sea turtles might not find a safe place to lay eggs...」。
27. What is a POSSIBLE result if a sea turtle eats a plastic bag? Inference
正確答案：C。第二段指出「If a turtle eats plastic, it may become very sick or even die.」這裡使用 may 表可能性。
28. Based on the passage, what is a DEFINITE (確定的) outcome if humans decrease plastic waste on land? Inference
正確答案：A。第三段明確說明：「...if we reduce plastic waste on land, we will protect these ancient creatures.」，will 表確定發生的積極結果。
29. What might happen if governments do NOT make stronger laws to prevent ocean pollution? Inference
正確答案：B。第三段最後提到「...if governments do not create stronger laws... some species of sea turtles could disappear completely...」。
30. Why did the author use the words "might", "may", and "could" throughout most of the article? Inference
正確答案：C。這些表達可能性的助動詞用來點出「潛在的未來威脅」，表示若人類此時採取行動改變，惡果就不一定會發生。

Part D: Sentence Completion (句子填空)

請依據句中提示的「確定程度(Certainty / Possibility)」，選出最符合語境的助動詞。

31. If you drop your trash on the floor, the teacher ________ be angry with you. (提示: 這是必然且確定的結果)
正確答案：B。提示註明是「必然且確定的結果」，在條件句的主要子句中應使用將來確定式助動詞 will。
32. If we keep the lights on all night, we ________ waste electricity. (提示: 根據科學事實，這是必然的後果)
正確答案：A。通宵開燈必然造成電力浪費，屬於極度確定的客觀事實，故用 will。
33. If we don't protect the polar bears, they ________ survive in the future. (提示: 100% 確定無法生存)
正確答案：B。提示強調「100% 確定無法生存」，表示確定否定的未來結果，應使用 won't。
34. If your family buys an electric car, you ________ reduce your carbon footprint, but it depends on how often you drive it. (提示: 有此可能性，但非绝对)
正確答案：B。由提示「有此可能性，但非絕對」及「depends on how often you drive」可知此結果帶有不確定性，應選 might。
35. If we don't clean the local park, people ________ want to go there anymore, though some people might not care. (提示: 表示不確定的否定預測)
正確答案：B。後句補充說明「有些人在意、有些人不在意」，表示這是一種不確定的否定預測，故選擇 might not 最為貼切。
