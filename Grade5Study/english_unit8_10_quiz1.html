Part II. Reading Comprehension 1 (Q26~Q30)
A Trip to the Nature Park
Last summer, Leo visited a famous nature park with his family. Before entering, the guide told them the safety rules. "Everyone has to stay inside the car," the guide said. "You can't open the windows, and you must drive carefully." Leo looked out the window and saw someone sitting near a tree. It was a park ranger watching the animals patiently. Suddenly, Leo saw something moving in the tall grass. It was a baby zebra! Leo wanted to take a picture. He turned to his sister and asked, "Could you hand me the camera, please?" Later, they stopped at a safe rest area. The guide offered them some snacks. "Would you like to try some traditional local biscuits?" asked the guide. Leo's father smiled and said, "Yes, I would. Thank you!" They enjoyed their lunch. They wanted to go somewhere quiet to rest after lunch, but there were many tourists anywhere they looked. However, it was an amazing trip.
Part III. Reading Comprehension 2 (Q31~Q35)
The Cultural Festival
Anna went to an international cultural festival in her city. The festival was huge, and there was music playing everywhere. She was looking for a special gift for her mother. She saw a booth selling beautiful handmade colorful blankets and bags. The shopkeeper saw Anna looking and asked, "Would you like a closer look at these cotton scarves?" Anna smiled and replied, "Yes, please." She touched a beautiful blue scarf. It felt soft and warm. She wondered about the price, so she said, "Could you tell me how much this costs, please?" The shopkeeper said it was twenty dollars. Anna didn't have anyone with her to ask for advice, but she knew her mother loved blue. She thought, "I should buy this for Mom's birthday." While she was paying, she noticed someone left a small notebook on the counter. She picked it up and asked the shopkeeper, "Did anyone lose this?" The shopkeeper thanked Anna for being honest and carefully put it away. Anna left the market happily with her perfect gift.
