Part I. Vocabulary and Grammar (40% - 每題 4 分)
1. What time ________ the safari park open on weekends?
💡 觀念解析：句尾有原形動詞 open（營業/開門），且主詞 the safari park 是單數，疑問句必須使用助動詞 **does**。不可選 is，因為 is 後面不能直接接原形動詞！
2. ________ the new documentary about endangered rhinos?
💡 觀念解析：此句主詞為 the new documentary，句中沒有一般普通動詞，about endangered rhinos 是介系詞片語。當句子是在詢問「狀態或身份」而非動作時，必須使用 Be 動詞 **Is**。
3. Our school will ________ a lot of money to the wildlife organization next week.
💡 觀念解析：助動詞 **will**（將會）後面，一定要接**原形動詞**。故選 (A) donate。
4. We are going to go ________ in the mountains next Saturday.
💡 觀念解析：去進行某項戶外休閒活動使用「**go + V-ing**」結構，例如：go camping（去露營）、go swimming（去游泳）。中間**絕對不能加 to**！
5. Why ________ that hunter look so suspicious near the forest?
💡 觀念解析：看到句中有一般動詞 **look**（看起來），主詞 that hunter 是單數，因此疑問句必須要派助動詞 **does** 出馬。
6. ________ the Bengal tiger an endangered species in Asia?
💡 觀念解析：後半句 an endangered species 是名詞片語（一種瀕危物種），全句沒有一般動詞，表示身分狀態，主詞是單數，故選 **Is**。
7. The morning train to the national park ________ at 7:15 a.m. sharp everyday.
💡 觀念解析：火車時刻表、官方固定行程，在英文中習慣用**現在簡單式**來表示。主詞 The morning train 為單數，動詞要加 s，故選 (C) leaves。
8. Scientists are going to ________ the rare plants in the jungle tomorrow afternoon.
💡 觀念解析：`be going to` 結構代表「即將、打算做某事」，後面的 `to` 是不定詞，後面必須接**原形動詞**。故選 (A) study。
9. If the government ________ stricter laws, the wild animals will die out.Challenge 進階題
💡 觀念解析：在 if 引導的條件句中，「現在式代替未來式」。If 子句的主詞 the government 是單數名詞，一般動詞的否定要用 **doesn't + 原形動詞**。
10. What time ________ the guide saying that the panda tour begins?Challenge 進階題
💡 觀念解析：注意看到關鍵字 **saying** (V-ing)！這是一個現在進行式的疑問句：「導遊『此時此刻正說著』幾點開始呢？」因此要用 Be 動詞 **is** 來與 V-ing 搭配。

Part III. Reading Comprehension (20% - 每題 5 分)
Mindy and Kevin are planning their summer vacation schedules. Mindy: Next month is July. My parents will take me to a famous panda conservation center in Sichuan. The center opens at 8:30 a.m. everyday, and we want to enter early to see the pandas eat bamboo. Kevin: Wow! That sounds wonderful. Does it cost a lot of money to go there? Mindy: No, the tickets are quite cheap. But we are going to donate some money to help the workers buy food for the baby pandas. What about you, Kevin? Kevin: Well, I don't have any travel plans. I think I will go swimming with my brother every morning. And our English summer camp starts on August 1st. Mindy: That's great! Don't forget to write postcards to me!
16. What time does the panda conservation center open?
💡 觀念解析：根據文章 Mindy 提到 "The center opens at 8:30 a.m. everyday"，故選 (B)。
17. Why is Mindy's family going to donate money?
💡 觀念解析：文章中寫到 "...donate some money to help the workers buy food for the baby pandas"，故選 (A)。
18. What will Kevin do every morning in July?
💡 觀念解析：Kevin 說 "I think I will go swimming with my brother every morning." 故答案為 (D)。注意 A 選項寫了錯誤語法 go to camping。
19. Which statement is TRUE according to the dialog?Challenge 進階題
💡 觀念解析：(A)文中說 tickets are quite cheap；(B)夏令營是在 August 1st；(D)Kevin沒有旅遊計畫。只有 (C) 符合文意 "enter early to see the pandas eat bamboo"。
Part IV. Change Sentences to Future Tense (12% - 每題 4 分)

範例：She is ten years old. (next year) → She will be ten years old next year.

20. Mr. Lin is a famous scientist. (in the future)
提示：注意 is 變成未來式助動詞後的變化型態。
💡 觀念解析：未來式可以使用 `will + 原形動詞`。**is** 的原形動詞是 **be**！
正確答案：
1. Mr. Lin will be a famous scientist in the future.
2. Mr. Lin is going to be a famous scientist in the future.
21. My sisters go swimming every summer. (next summer)
提示：注意去游泳的固定片語寫法，不要加上多餘的介系詞。
💡 觀念解析：未來式 `will + 原形動詞`。原本的動詞 go 已經是原形，後面直接連用 swimming。絕不可寫成意圖錯誤的 "go to swimming"！
正確答案：
1. My sisters will go swimming next summer.
2. My sisters are going to go swimming next summer.
22. The plane lands at 3:00 p.m. every day. (tomorrow afternoon)
提示：改為未來式時，原本單數動詞字尾的 -s 必須去掉還原。
💡 觀念解析：原本的 lands 是因為單數主詞加了 s。改為 will 後，必須還原成原形動詞 **land**。
正確答案：
1. The plane will land at 3:00 p.m. tomorrow afternoon.
2. The plane is going to land at 3:00 p.m. tomorrow afternoon.
Part V. Write the Questions (8% - 每題 4 分)

⚠️ 核心魔王關卡：請仔細分辨何時該用 "is"，何時該用 "does"！

23. 答：The zoo opens at 8:30 a.m.
問：____________________________________________________ (請造出 What time 引導的問句)
檢查重點：原句有普通動詞 opens，做成問句要借用哪一個助動詞？後面的動詞有還原嗎？
💡 觀念解析：答案句有一般動作動詞 **opens**，主詞 the zoo 是單數。因此詢問時間的問句必須使用助動詞 **does**，並且將動詞還原成 **open**。
正確答案：What time does the zoo open?
24. 答：The next safari tour is at 10:00 a.m.
問：____________________________________________________ (請造出 What time 引導的問句)
檢查重點：原句使用的是 "is"（狀態/時間點），並沒有其他普通動作動詞。問句該用什麼？
💡 觀念解析：答案句中使用的是 Be 動詞 **is**（後面直接接時間 at 10:00 a.m.），裡面**沒有**任何普通的動作動詞。因此造問句時，直接把 Be 動詞提到前面即可！
正確答案：What time is the next safari tour?
