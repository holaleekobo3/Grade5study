Part III. Reading Comprehension. (20% - 每題 4 分)
Tina and Susan both love shopping and always go together to the shopping mall.

Tina: Chinese New Year is coming. If I get a lot of red envelope money, I'll buy a new reusable water bottle. Things at the eco-shop on Oak Street are very good.
Susan: Me too! If I get enough money, I'll take you to that street vendor on the corner. They sell beautiful shopping bags made of cloth. They are so cheap. If we use cloth bags, we won't need plastic bags anymore.
Tina: Really! Could you take me there this afternoon so we can have a look?
Susan: I'm sorry. If I have a piano lesson this afternoon, I won't have time. How about next Saturday?
Tina: That sounds great! If it rains next Saturday, we can still go because the vendor has a big umbrella.
16. What do Tina and Susan both love to do?
17. Why will Susan not go to the street vendor this afternoon?
18. What is true about the shopping bags at the street vendor?
19. What will happen if they use cloth bags?
20. Which statement is TRUE based on the dialogue?
