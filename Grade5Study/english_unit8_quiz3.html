Unit 9 核心文法專題特訓測驗

重點聚焦：Lesson 2 (Would you like...) & Lesson 6 (Could you please...) | 總計 35 題

Part A: 核心文法應用 (Multiple Choice 1-25)
💡 文法提醒：
1. Would you like + 名詞 (想要某物) / Would you like + to + 原形動詞 (想要做某事)。
2. Could you + 原形動詞 (+ please)? 用於客氣地請求他人協助。
Part B: 情境閱讀理解一 (Reading Comprehension 1)
Welcome to the Chichicastenango Market Helper! Are you looking for beautiful traditional clothes or colorful blankets made of high-quality cotton? Our market has everything you need! If you are a visitor, you might feel confused about the prices or sizes. Don't worry! You can always use these polite expressions to talk to our local shopkeepers. When you see a nice jacket on a high shelf, you can say: "Could you please take it down for me? I would like to try it on in the fitting room." If you think the price is a bit too expensive, you can politely ask: "Could you please show me a cheaper one?" Most shopkeepers are very friendly. They will welcome you and ask: "Would you like to buy some special handmade crafts as gifts for your family? We accept both local money and international dollars!" Remember, being polite always makes your travel experience much happier and healthier!
Part C: 情境閱讀理解二 (Reading Comprehension 2)
An Evening Conversation in Antigua Mia and Luke are walking through a quiet street in Antigua after a long day of exploring. They stop in front of a small cafe that sells traditional Guatemalan hot chocolate. Cafe Owner: Good evening! You look a bit tired and cold from the night wind. Would you like a warm table inside by the window? Mia: Yes, please. That sounds very cozy. Luke: Excuse me, sir. Could you please show us the menu? We would like to see what traditional snacks you have. Cafe Owner: Certainly! Here is the menu. Today, we have special sweet bread. Would you like to try some? It goes perfectly with our hot chocolate. Mia: Oh, I would love to! It looks delicious in the picture. Luke: Could you please bring us two cups of hot chocolate and one plate of sweet bread? Cafe Owner: Of course! Please wait for a few minutes. I will bring them to your table right away. Enjoy your evening! Mia: Thank you. People here are so polite and caring. It always makes me smile!
